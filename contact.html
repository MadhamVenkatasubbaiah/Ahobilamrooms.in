Terms & Guidelines

Please read our house rules and booking policies before confirming your stay.

  • Check-in & Check-out

    Standard Check-in time is 12:00 PM and Check-out time is 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out is subject to room availability and may incur additional charges.

  • Mandatory ID Proof

    All adult guests must present a valid Government-issued ID card (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Driving License, or Passport) at the time of check-in. PAN cards are not accepted as valid address proof.

  • Sacred Town Guidelines (Strictly Followed)

    As Ahobilam is a sacred pilgrimage destination, the consumption of alcohol, non-vegetarian food, and smoking are strictly prohibited inside the rooms and within the property premises.

  • Booking & Cancellation

    An advance payment is required to confirm your booking. Cancellations made 48 hours prior to the check-in date will be fully refunded. Late cancellations or no-shows are non-refundable.

  • Property Damage & Liability

    Guests will be held responsible for any loss or damage to hotel property caused by themselves or their visitors. The management is not responsible for the loss of any personal valuables left in the rooms.
