Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about visiting Ahobilam and our booking policies.

1. How much time does it take to visit all Nava Narasimha temples?

It typically takes 2 full days to complete the darshan of all 9 temples. Day 1 is usually dedicated to Lower Ahobilam and nearby accessible temples, while Day 2 requires trekking in Upper Ahobilam to visit Ugra Stambham, Jwala, and Pavana Narasimha temples.

2. Is trekking required? Can elders and children do it?

Yes, trekking is mandatory for a few temples in Upper Ahobilam. Lower Ahobilam temples are easily accessible by road. For difficult treks like Ugra Stambham and Jwala Narasimha, it might be tough for elders and young children. However, local Doli services (palanquins) are available for hire.

3. Which mobile networks work best in Ahobilam?

Ahobilam is situated in a dense forest area (Nallamala hills). Currently, BSNL and Jio networks work reasonably well. Other networks like Airtel and Vi may have poor or no connectivity, especially in Upper Ahobilam.

4. Are local transport and food facilities available?

Yes. Local Mahindra Jeeps are available for hire to reach temples located deep in the forest (like Pavana and Bhargava Narasimha). For food, basic vegetarian meals are available at local eateries, and Nitya Annadanam (free meals) is provided by the Devasthanam and local Mutts.

5. Do the rooms have AC and hot water facilities?

Yes! AT Madam Rajeswari Complex & Veerabadhra Complex offer AC and Non-AC room options. All rooms are equipped with attached bathrooms and geysers for 24/7 hot water to ensure a comfortable stay after your trekking.

6. Why is it mandatory to pay an advance for booking?

Ahobilam is a remote pilgrimage destination with limited premium accommodation. Paying an advance confirms your serious intent to visit and allows us to block the room exclusively for you. This prevents last-minute cancellations that could negatively affect other devotees looking for rooms.

7. Should I pay the full amount in advance or just a booking amount?

You don't need to pay the full amount. A partial advance (typically 50%) is sufficient to confirm and hold your room. The remaining balance can be cleared directly at the property during check-in. However, paying the full amount in advance ensures a quicker, hassle-free check-in process.

8. What is your cancellation and refund policy?

We understand that plans can change. If you cancel your booking at least 48 hours before your scheduled check-in date, we will process a Full Refund of your advance amount. The refund will be credited back to your original payment method within 2-3 working days.

9. Under what situations is the advance amount strictly non-refundable?

The advance amount becomes completely Non-Refundable if the cancellation is made less than 48 hours before the check-in time, or in the case of a "No-Show" (failing to arrive without prior intimation). This policy compensates for the blocked room that could have been provided to other waiting devotees.
